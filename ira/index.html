What we've studied

Writing Studio (Lessons 1–4) and Speaking Studio (Lesson 5), on our Miro board.

1

Text Me Back

Text messages and chat

Reading a real chat for the main idea and details; 30+ text abbreviations (BTW, TBH, ASAP, ETA, L8R); what makes a good reply; who you can send text-speak to; decoding and writing short messages.

Key language: WYD rn? · HBU? · IDK · TTYL · CU Sat · np

2

Sincerely Yours

Formal emails

Formal, semi-formal and informal register; the same news as a text and as an email; rules for formal writing (no abbreviations, full forms, clear subject line); polite requests; fixing a badly written email; email ping-pong.

Key language: I am writing to… · Could you please…? · I look forward to hearing from you. · Kind regards

3

Caption This

Instagram captions for your hair studio

Stopping the scroll; the anatomy of a caption (hook, story, call to action, hashtags); hooks; writing tips; words that sell; fixing a caption; writing your own salon posts in English.

Key language: Okay, real talk… · Save this for your next salon visit. · Tell me in the comments…

4

A Year in Writing

Revision: one story, three styles

Writing about your birthday, your year and your goals as a text, an email and a caption; present perfect for "this year" vs past simple for finished time; spend time + -ing; manage / hope / plan + to; making sentences sound B2.

Key language: So far, I have… · I haven't… yet · I finally managed to… · I hope to… · Although… / However…

5

And the Award Goes To

Speaking: giving an interview and an award speech

Reading a winner's magazine interview; an interview toolkit (buy time, be honest, give an example, say thank you, show feelings); A + R + E answers; award vocabulary with word stress; stress patterns; pauses, stress and falling intonation in a short speech; answering interview questions in one minute.

Key language: That's a good question. · I'd say… · It hasn't sunk in yet. · I'm over the moon. · I'm deeply honoured. · I couldn't have done it without…

Error corrections

What you said in class, and what to say instead.

  • 15 Octoberceremony (wrong stress)→CER-e-mo-ny /ˈserəməni/Stress on the first syllable.
  • 15 OctoberI'm tired about…→I'm tired of…tired of + noun / -ing: "I'm tired of waiting."
  • 15 Octoberon rush→in a rushFixed phrase: "Sorry, I'm in a rush."
  • 15 OctoberI'm in stress→I'm stressed / distressedUse the adjective for how you feel.
  • 26 Octoberits / it's mixed up→its = його/її · it's = it is"The dog wagged its tail." / "It's cold today."
  • 2 NovemberI'm a person who don't…→I'm a person who doesn't…"A person" = he/she, so the verb takes -s: doesn't.
  • 2 Novemberin the same point→at the same timeFixed phrase: at the same time = одночасно.
  • 26 NovemberI was success→It was successfulUse the adjective: it was successful. "I was a success" means you as a person were a success.
  • 26 Novembermore faster→fasterShort adjectives take -er without more: faster, bigger, easier.
  • 26 Novemberrecognition (stress)→rec-og-NI-tion /ˌrekəɡˈnɪʃn/Stress on NI.
  • 26 Novembersure (pronunciation)→/ʃɔː/ in British EnglishIt sounds like "shore".

  • You write captions for your own hair posts, which is the most useful writing practice you can do.

What to focus on

  • He / she / a person + verb-sIn lessons: "I'm a person who don't…"After he, she, it and a person, check the verb: doesn't, has, likes, works.
  • Fixed phrases with prepositionsIn lessons: "tired about", "on rush", "in the same point"Learn them as one block: tired of, in a rush, at the same time. Practise them in the "From our lessons" flashcards.
  • Adjectives for feelingsIn lessons: "I'm in stress"Use I'm + adjective: I'm stressed, I'm relaxed, I'm exhausted, I'm distressed.
  • its vs it's in writingIn lessons: mixed up in your writingBefore you send a text or a caption, read it once more: can you say "it is"? Then write it's.
  • Verb patterns from Lesson 4In lessons: the key B2 grammar for writing about your yearhope to / manage to / plan to + verb; spend time + -ing; past simple with finished time (last month, in June).
  • Word stress in new wordsIn lessons: "ceremony"Tap Listen on each new word in the flashcards and repeat the stressed syllable louder.
