Romanticizing Winter

Reading lesson · B2–C1 · about 60 minutes
Aims. Main: reading for gist and for specific detail in an informal blog article about embracing winter. Subsidiary: descriptive vocabulary and idioms from the text (embrace, insulation, ambiance, worth every penny…). Productive: fluency practice discussing winter habits and creating a “cosy winter guide” for their own city.

Anticipated problems. The text is long (~1,300 words): set clear time limits; for gist, Ss read only the intro, the 7 headings and the closing. Gezelligheid is unfamiliar: model /xəˈzɛləxhɛit/ roughly; approximations are fine. Informal idioms (keep on keeping on, pull up a chair): deal with them in Task 6 through context, not before reading.

Stage 1Lead-in

5 min · T–S, S–S
Aim: to generate interest in the topic and activate schemata. Brief open-class feedback; board any useful language Ss produce.

Look at the pictures and discuss in pairs.

  1. How do you feel when winter starts: excited, neutral or a bit miserable? Why?
  2. What do people in your country usually do to survive the cold, dark months?
  3. What does “romanticizing” something mean? Can you romanticize winter?

Stage 2Key words

7 min · S, S–S
Aim: to pre-teach words Ss need to understand the gist.
CCQs: If you use the heating sparingly, is your flat very warm? (No) · If a café has a nice ambiance, is that about the food or the feeling? (the feeling)
Drill: ambiance /ˈæmbiəns/, insulation /ˌɪnsjuˈleɪʃn/.
